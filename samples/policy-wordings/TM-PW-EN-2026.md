# Thames Mutual Insurance: Engineering and Plant Property Wording (2026 edition)

> Synthetic wording written for a portfolio project. Thames Mutual Insurance is fictional. This is not a real
> policy and must not be relied on for cover.

## 1. Insuring clause

### 1.1 Cover
We will pay for loss of or damage to the Insured Plant at the Premises, happening during the Period of Insurance and caused by an Insured Peril listed in Section 3, subject to the Exclusions in Section 4, the Conditions in Section 5 and the Excess in Section 6.

### 1.2 Basis of settlement
We will pay the cost of repairing the damaged plant. If it cannot be repaired economically we will pay its replacement cost, less an allowance for wear and tear based on its age and condition. We will not pay more than the sum insured shown in the Schedule for the item.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Insured Plant
The machinery, presses, lathes, welding and fabrication equipment, compressors, hydraulic systems and tooling described in the Schedule.

### 2.2 Breakdown
Sudden and unforeseen physical damage to Insured Plant that needs immediate repair or replacement before it can be used again, caused by mechanical or electrical failure, including failure of a hydraulic line, seal or pump.

### 2.3 Unoccupied
A workshop is Unoccupied when nobody has been present in it for business purposes, and it has not been used for business, for more than 30 consecutive days.

## 3. Insured perils

### 3.1 Breakdown
Loss or damage to Insured Plant caused by Breakdown, including the cost of the specialist engineers needed to diagnose and repair it.

### 3.2 Fire, lightning and explosion
Loss or damage caused by fire, lightning or explosion, including the bursting of a pressure vessel or steam boiler.

### 3.3 Escape of water
Loss or damage caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed tank or pipe at the Premises.

### 3.4 Theft
Loss or damage caused by theft or attempted theft involving forcible and violent entry to or exit from the Premises.

### 3.5 Collapse and overturning
Loss or damage caused by the collapse, overturning or falling of Insured Plant, including lifting equipment.

## 4. Exclusions

### 4.1 Wear and tear
We will not pay for the cost of replacing any part that has failed because of wear and tear, gradual deterioration, fatigue, corrosion, erosion or scale, or for routine maintenance. We will pay for sudden damage to other parts that follows from such a failure.

### 4.2 Flood
We will not pay for loss or damage caused by flood, meaning inundation of the Premises by surface water, overflowing rivers or streams, the sea, or rising groundwater.

### 4.3 Unoccupied workshops
We will not pay for loss or damage by escape of water, theft or malicious damage to Insured Plant in a workshop that has been Unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days at the date of loss.

### 4.4 Testing and overloading
We will not pay for loss or damage to Insured Plant caused by testing it beyond its rated capacity, or by operating it while it is known to be defective.

### 4.5 Deliberate acts
We will not pay for loss or damage caused deliberately by you, your partners, directors or employees, or by anyone acting with their consent.

## 5. Conditions

### 5.1 Notification of claims
You must tell us about any loss that may lead to a claim as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event within 21 days of the date of loss. If you notify us later than this we may refuse the claim to the extent that the delay has prejudiced our investigation.

### 5.2 Maintenance and inspection
You must keep Insured Plant in good working order, maintain it as its manufacturer recommends, and keep records of inspection and servicing, which we may ask to see when a claim is made.

### 5.3 Fraud
If a claim is made that is dishonest or exaggerated in any respect, we will not pay any part of that claim and may cancel the policy from the date of the fraudulent act.

## 6. Excess and limits

### 6.1 Excess
We will deduct the excess shown in the Schedule from every claim. For Breakdown the excess is applied to each item of Insured Plant separately.

### 6.2 Sum insured
We will not pay more than the sum insured for any one item of Insured Plant, and no more than the total sum insured shown in the Schedule for all claims in the Period of Insurance.
