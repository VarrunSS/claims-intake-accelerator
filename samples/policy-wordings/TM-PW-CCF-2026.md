# Thames Mutual Insurance: Commercial Combined Property Wording with Flood Extension (2026 edition)

> Synthetic wording written for a portfolio project. Thames Mutual Insurance is fictional. This is not a real
> policy and must not be relied on for cover.

## 1. Insuring clause

### 1.1 Cover
We will pay for loss of or damage to the Property Insured at the Premises, happening during the Period of Insurance and caused by an Insured Peril listed in Section 3, subject to the Exclusions in Section 4, the Conditions in Section 5 and the Excess in Section 6.

### 1.2 Basis of settlement
We will pay the cost of repairing or replacing the damaged property, less an allowance for wear and tear where the property is machinery, plant or stock more than five years old. We will not pay more than the sum insured shown in the Schedule for the item.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Premises
The buildings, yards and open-air display areas at the addresses shown in the Schedule.

### 2.2 Property Insured
The buildings, contents, machinery, plant, stock and outdoor stock described in the Schedule, up to the sum insured for each item.

### 2.3 Unoccupied
A building is Unoccupied when nobody has been present in it for business purposes, and it has not been used for business, for more than 30 consecutive days. Normal seasonal closure of less than 30 days is not Unoccupied.

### 2.4 Flood
Inundation of the Premises by surface water, overflowing rivers or streams, the sea, or rising groundwater, or by water that enters a building because of heavy rainfall overwhelming drains.

## 3. Insured perils

### 3.1 Fire, lightning and explosion
Loss or damage caused by fire, lightning or explosion, including smoke damage and the cost of fire brigade attendance.

### 3.2 Escape of water
Loss or damage caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed water tank, apparatus or pipe, including a burst or leaking pipe.

### 3.3 Theft
Loss or damage caused by theft or attempted theft involving forcible and violent entry to or exit from the Premises, or assault or threat of violence at the Premises.

### 3.4 Storm
Loss or damage caused by storm, meaning wind of gale force or a violent fall of rain, hail or snow, to buildings that are in good repair.

### 3.5 Flood extension
Loss or damage caused by Flood, as defined in clause 2.4, up to a limit of £250,000 for any one event. This extension applies only because it is shown as operative in the Schedule.

### 3.6 Refrigeration failure
Loss of refrigerated stock caused by a sudden failure of refrigeration plant at the Premises, provided the plant was serviced within the last 12 months.

## 4. Exclusions

### 4.1 Wear and tear
We will not pay for loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, rot, mould, or a defect that develops over time. We will pay for sudden damage that follows from wear and tear if the damage is itself caused by an Insured Peril.

### 4.2 Unoccupied buildings
We will not pay for loss or damage by escape of water, theft or malicious damage to any building that has been Unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days at the date of loss, unless we agreed in writing to the unoccupancy.

### 4.3 Deliberate acts
We will not pay for loss or damage caused deliberately by you, your partners, directors or employees, or by anyone acting with their consent.

### 4.4 War and terrorism
We will not pay for loss or damage caused by war, invasion, civil commotion, or terrorism, except where the law requires us to cover it.

## 5. Conditions

### 5.1 Notification of claims
You must tell us about any loss that may lead to a claim as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event within 30 days of the date of loss. If you notify us later than this we may refuse the claim to the extent that the delay has prejudiced our investigation.

### 5.2 Reasonable care
You must take all reasonable steps to protect the Property Insured from loss or damage, including moving stock away from areas at known risk of flood when a flood warning is in force.

### 5.3 Fraud
If a claim is made that is dishonest or exaggerated in any respect, we will not pay any part of that claim and may cancel the policy from the date of the fraudulent act.

### 5.4 Co-operation
You must give us the information, documents and access we reasonably ask for to investigate and settle a claim. You must not admit liability or negotiate a settlement without our agreement.

## 6. Excess and limits

### 6.1 Excess
We will deduct the excess shown in the Schedule from every claim. Where one event causes loss under more than one Insured Peril, only one excess applies.

### 6.2 Flood excess
For loss caused by Flood the excess is increased to £2,500 or the excess shown in the Schedule, whichever is higher.
