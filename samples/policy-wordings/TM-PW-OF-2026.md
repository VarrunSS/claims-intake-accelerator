# Thames Mutual Insurance: Office Package Property Wording (2026 edition)

> Synthetic wording written for a portfolio project. Thames Mutual Insurance is fictional. This is not a real
> policy and must not be relied on for cover.

## 1. Insuring clause

### 1.1 Cover
We will pay for loss of or damage to the Office Contents and any Landlord's Fixtures you are responsible for, at the Premises, happening during the Period of Insurance and caused by an Insured Peril listed in Section 3, subject to the Exclusions in Section 4, the Conditions in Section 5 and the Excess in Section 6.

### 1.2 Basis of settlement
We will pay the cost of repairing or replacing the damaged property with property of the same kind, less an allowance for wear and tear. We will not pay more than the sum insured shown in the Schedule for the item.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Premises
The office suite or building at the address shown in the Schedule.

### 2.2 Office Contents
Furniture, fixtures and fittings, computers and telecommunications equipment, stationery, documents and records, and the stock of the business, at the Premises.

### 2.3 Unoccupied
The Premises are Unoccupied when nobody has been present at them for business purposes, and they have not been used for business, for more than 30 consecutive days.

## 3. Insured perils

### 3.1 Fire, lightning and explosion
Loss or damage caused by fire, lightning or explosion, including smoke damage and the cost of fire brigade attendance.

### 3.2 Escape of water
Loss or damage caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed water tank, apparatus or pipe, including a burst or leaking pipe, and by the accidental overflow of sprinklers.

### 3.3 Theft
Loss or damage caused by theft or attempted theft involving forcible and violent entry to or exit from the Premises.

### 3.4 Storm
Loss or damage caused by storm, meaning wind of gale force or a violent fall of rain, hail or snow, to buildings that are in good repair.

### 3.5 Accidental damage to computers
Accidental damage to computers and telecommunications equipment at the Premises, including damage caused by liquid spilled on the equipment.

## 4. Exclusions

### 4.1 Flood
We will not pay for loss or damage caused by flood, meaning inundation of the Premises by surface water, overflowing rivers or streams, the sea, or rising groundwater, or by water that enters the Premises because of heavy rainfall overwhelming drains. Basement and ground-floor offices are not insured against flood under this wording.

### 4.2 Wear and tear
We will not pay for loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, rot or mould, or a defect that develops over time, or for the cost of maintaining equipment.

### 4.3 Unoccupied premises
We will not pay for loss or damage by escape of water, theft or malicious damage if the Premises have been Unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days at the date of loss, unless we agreed in writing to the unoccupancy.

### 4.4 Deliberate acts
We will not pay for loss or damage caused deliberately by you, your partners, directors or employees, or by anyone acting with their consent.

## 5. Conditions

### 5.1 Notification of claims
You must tell us about any loss that may lead to a claim as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event within 14 days of the date of loss. If you notify us later than this we may refuse the claim to the extent that the delay has prejudiced our investigation.

### 5.2 Reasonable care
You must take all reasonable steps to protect the Office Contents from loss or damage, including keeping the Premises locked and the alarm set when they are not in use.

### 5.3 Fraud
If a claim is made that is dishonest or exaggerated in any respect, we will not pay any part of that claim and may cancel the policy from the date of the fraudulent act.

## 6. Excess and limits

### 6.1 Excess
We will deduct the excess shown in the Schedule from every claim.

### 6.2 Documents and records
We will pay no more than £25,000 for the cost of reconstituting documents and records, whatever the cause of the loss.
